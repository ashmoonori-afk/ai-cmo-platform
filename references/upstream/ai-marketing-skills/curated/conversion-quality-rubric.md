Score four dimensions from 0 to 25 for a total of 100. Apply the rubric to a
static review artifact; it does not authorize changes to any live surface.

## Value statement

- The intended audience and offered value are immediately understandable.
- Specific language connects the offer to a real need.
- Urgency, when present, is truthful and proportionate.

## Clarity and friction

- The requested next step is unambiguous.
- The information path has no unnecessary distractions or steps.
- Expectations established by the referring context remain consistent.
- Required effort is disclosed before commitment.

## Proof and trust

- Evidence is specific, attributable, and relevant to the claim.
- Testimonials and examples are not presented more broadly than warranted.
- Trust indicators are genuine and current.
- Scarcity and deadlines are not manufactured.

## Action clarity

- One primary next action is visually and verbally clear.
- Action wording states what the person receives or does.
- Supporting text resolves material uncertainty.
- Competing actions do not obscure the intended path.
