Score four dimensions from 0 to 25 for a total of 100. Record evidence for each
score; the number alone is not a useful review.

## Opening strength

- Establishes a specific reason to continue.
- Creates relevant curiosity without misleading the reader.
- States a concrete tension, result, or point of view.

## Voice credibility

- Sounds human and appropriate to the stated author.
- Uses concrete details and avoids empty jargon.
- Supports strong claims with evidence.
- Maintains a consistent point of view.

## Value density

- Each section contributes to the purpose.
- Claims are specific rather than vague.
- The reader gains an applicable idea or decision aid.
- Repetition and filler are removed.

## Response potential

- The material is worth saving, sharing, or discussing.
- Any response invitation is genuine and relevant.
- Structure and formatting fit the intended reading context.
- Debate is grounded in a substantive claim rather than provocation alone.
