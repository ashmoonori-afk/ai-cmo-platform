Score four dimensions from 0 to 25 for a total of 100. Explain uncertainty and
retain dissent instead of manufacturing consensus.

## Evidence quality

- Material claims have verifiable support.
- Examples include enough context, outcome, and timing to be assessed.
- Unsupported assertions are labeled or excluded.
- Adverse evidence and warning signs remain visible.

## Criteria relevance

- Criteria match the actual decision and context.
- Weight reflects importance rather than convenience.
- Context differences are explicit.
- Disqualifying conditions are considered.

## Risk assessment

- Unknowns and evidence gaps are named.
- Material risks include plausible mitigations.
- Alternatives and a relevant baseline are considered.
- Confidence matches the quality of available evidence.

## Decision usefulness

- The recommendation is clear and bounded.
- The rationale can be reconstructed from the criteria.
- A next decision point is identified.
- A materially different reviewer view is preserved.
