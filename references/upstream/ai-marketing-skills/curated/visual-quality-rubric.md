Score four dimensions from 0 to 25 for a total of 100. Review at the expected
display size and retain the underlying evidence for factual checks.

## Data integrity

- Values match the cited source.
- Labels, units, axes, scales, and time windows are accurate.
- Framing does not rely on cherry-picking or misleading truncation.
- The source and relevant caveats are visible.

## Visual clarity

- The principal point is apparent quickly.
- Text remains readable in the intended context.
- Meaning does not depend on color alone.
- Decorative elements do not compete with the evidence.

## Insight delivery

- The form supports a decision-relevant story rather than merely displaying data.
- The title communicates the takeaway.
- Annotations direct attention to the important comparison.
- Supporting context is sufficient to avoid a misleading interpretation.

## Design quality

- Typography, color, alignment, and spacing are consistent.
- Styling suits the audience and subject.
- Visual hierarchy makes comparisons easy.
- The result remains legible at compact sizes.
