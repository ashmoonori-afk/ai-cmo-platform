Score four dimensions from 0 to 25 for a total of 100. Any return threshold
should be selected for the decision context rather than treated as universal.

## Data foundation

- Claims rely on observed evidence rather than unsupported projections.
- Sources can be verified.
- Figures are specific, current enough, and properly scoped.
- Assumptions are distinguished from observations.

## Actionability

- The proposed next decision or test is clear.
- Timing and resource assumptions are realistic.
- Ownership and dependencies are identified.
- The scope is small enough to evaluate.

## Return clarity

- Expected benefit and cost use comparable units.
- The basis and uncertainty of estimates are explicit.
- Alternatives, including no change, are compared.
- The evaluation window is appropriate to the expected effect.

## Risk assessment

- Failure modes and downside are stated candidly.
- Mitigations correspond to identified risks.
- Critical dependencies and reversibility are visible.
- Stop conditions are defined before action.
